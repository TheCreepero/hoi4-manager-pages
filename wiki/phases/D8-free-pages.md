# Phase D8: Free pages

A scripted-page phase of Milestone D ([Roadmap](../Roadmap.md)): National Figures and Army Readiness stop docking to their tabs and become windows of their own, like the Records window, so that they no longer need the `clipping = no` line of this mod's politics and recruit-deploy overrides. Opened on 2026-10-21 with `/phase D8`, as the first step of the split of the scripted GUIs into their own mod (HOI4 Manager Pages, standalone; user decision of 2026-10-21).

## Scope

- Vanilla file and windows: none overridden by this phase. `mod1440_figures` (docked to `politics_tab`, `interface/mod1440_figures.gui`), `mod1440_army_readiness` and `mod1440_army_readiness_body` (docked to `deployment_tab`, `interface/mod1440_army_readiness.gui`), their handles, all generated by `tools/build_managers.py`.
- What the current build hides: both pages are drawn only because `interface/zz_1440p_politics.gui:16` and `interface/zz_1440p_recruit_deploy.gui:14-16` set `clipping = no` on the vanilla windows (Engine Facts X6). Over vanilla's windows, as the standalone pages mod will be loaded, the pages and the Army Readiness handle (at x 1256, outside the 550 px panel) are cut off.
- The user's decision (2026-10-21, from three options): "Drop the two docked pages' dependency: rework National Figures and Army Readiness so they no longer dock to a tab (free windows like Records)." The alternative of the pages mod listing the screens mod as a dependency was not taken.
- Out of scope: the content and look of the pages; the States page (the vanilla Construction window already lets a page out); the split itself (the plan `would-it-be-difficult-nested-hopcroft.md` in the user's plans folder, steps 2 to 6) and the removal of the two `clipping = no` lines, which follows the game check of this phase.
