# Phase E7: Portraits through the game

Opened on 2026-10-29 with `/pages-phase`, after E4's redesign round. The user: portraits occasionally missing "has been plaguing several parts of this mod for a while now"; wanted a way where "any mod (current or future) will automatically be supported" and "portraits are reliably correct and not missing".

## Scope

- **Why portraits go missing today:** no text function gives a character's portrait [SGUI-028], so the pages carry a generated table of character and sprite (`tools/portraits_table.py`). It misses characters of mods not in the table, portraits given as texture files (Road to 56's 1451 [RT56-007]), generated characters, and goes stale after a game update.
- **What the Manager's investigation of 2026-10-29 found** (read in `hoi4.exe` 1.19.3, not yet seen in the game): `properties = { <icon> = { image = "portrait" } }` draws the scope's unit leader portrait through the game's own portrait code, sprite or texture file, generated or not [SGUI-053]; no loc function gives a portrait [LOC-014]; a character with no unit leader role is expected to draw nothing.
- **Step 1, the probe:** a throwaway scripted GUI that confirms [SGUI-053] in one launch with Road to 56 loaded; removed afterwards.
- **Step 2, if it holds:** every portrait of a general, admiral or operative goes through `image = "portrait"` (Officers, Army Readiness, War Diary, state card, the commanders of a war, the Government page's officers and high command where they are unit leaders); single slots become one-row lists. Heads of government and advisors keep the table and the flag fallback.
- **Out of scope:** a civilian's portrait without the table (nothing in the engine gives it); a patch of `hoi4.exe`.

## Roles and handoffs

- Handoff folder: `scratch/phases/E7/` (not in the repository)
