# Phase D9: National Figures refinement

A scripted-page phase of Milestone D ([Roadmap](../Roadmap.md)): a redesign and refinement round of the National Figures page (with its History graph) after the user had played with the D4 round 2 build and the free window of D8. Opened on 2026-10-22 with `/pages-phase`. The user's words:

> I'd like a redesign/refinement round of the National Figures view. Few pointers:
> - Casualties are not tracked as "overall casualties" like I expect to. If I suffer casualties in a war, and the war ends, the amount of casualties suffered during that war disappear from the graph.
> - I'd also like a second casualties data: Casualties suffered each month. Right now the casualties are additive, I also want to be able to see things like "which month was the most deadly for me".
> - There is no support for negative traits. For example: I often have negative infantry equipment balance, but that is not represented at all.
> - Any other UI/UX improvements you can imagine
> - Any new data that would make sense to show here.

## Scope

- Vanilla file and windows: none overridden. The page is `mod1440_figures` (`interface/mod1440_figures.gui`, a free window at 550,78 of 1680x1210 since D8), its handle `mod1440_figures_handle` on the politics tab, the monthly recording `mod1440_h_record` and the 31 series `mod1440_h_<n>`, all generated by the section "National Figures and History (D4, D5)" of `tools/build_managers.py`.
- What the current build does badly (from the user's game): the Casualties row and series read `casualties_k`, which counts the wars the country is in now, so a war's dead vanish from the table and the graph when the war ends; casualties have no per-month series, so the deadliest month cannot be seen; a figure that stands at zero because the army is short of it (infantry equipment in stock) shows 0, not the deficit, and the page has no row that can go negative except the resource balances.
- Out of scope: the other pages, the top bar strip, the Records; the page's place on the screen and its handle (D8); the docking question.

What vanilla does badly: The Casualties row and series 10 read the game's `casualties_k`, which counts only the wars in progress: when a war ends its dead vanish from the table and the graph falls back to 0 (the user's game; the current figure of the casualties group shows it, with "In a year" in green because the number fell). There is no series of the dead per month, so the deadliest month cannot be seen. Infantry equipment shows the stock, which is 0 while the army is short: a deficit reads the same as a balanced stock. Sparklines and the graph draw a negative month as a short green column from the lowest month, and Highest and Lowest give their month only in a tooltip.

**Casualties: a total that never falls (series 31).** A daily step `mod1440_h_cas_step` (player only, from `on_daily`, and once more at the start of `mod1440_h_record`) reads `t = casualties_k` and compares it with `mod1440_h_cas_last`, the value it saw the day before. A rise `d = t - cas_last > 0` is added to `mod1440_h_cas_sum` (the total) and to `mod1440_h_cas_mon` (the month so far); a fall (a war ended, a peace) adds nothing and only moves the baseline: `cas_last = t`. On the first of each month `mod1440_h_record` writes `cas_sum` into the new series **31, "Casualties, all wars"**, in thousands like the game's value. The total never falls.

**What is lost:** with the daily step, only the dead of one day: when a war ends on the same day as another war's dead rise, the net change masks the rise; and a war that starts and ends within one day. Wars that ended before the mod's first month are not in the game's value and cannot be counted. With a monthly step alone (the contract's wording) a war that ended inside a month would lose its dead since the last first, and a war that started and ended between two firsts would vanish: see decision 3.

**An older save** (no `mod1440_h_cas_ver`): the first run of `mod1440_h_record` or `mod1440_h_draw` back-fills 31 and 32 from series 10, which every save has: the first month of 31 is series 10's first month, then each month adds the rise of series 10 since the month before (a fall adds nothing); the first month of 32 is -1 (no column), every later month the rise. Then `cas_sum` is the last back-filled month plus any rise of `casualties_k` since, `cas_last = casualties_k`, `cas_ver = 1`. One loop of at most 120 steps, once per save. The back-filled months have the monthly losses named above; the tooltip of the total says from which date the count is daily (`mod1440_h_cas_from`).

**Casualties: the dead of each month (series 32) and the deadliest month.** On the first of each month `mod1440_h_record` writes `cas_mon` (the dead since the record before) into series **32, "Casualties in the month"**, then sets it to 0; the first record of a game writes -1 (no month before it). The column dated 1 July 1940 holds June's dead.
