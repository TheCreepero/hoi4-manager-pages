# Engine Facts

What the mod relies on about how the Clausewitz engine treats its files, one row per fact, with how sure we are. [Design Rules](Design-Rules.md) explains the rules that follow from these facts; this page is what an agent scans before proposing a layout or asking for a launch.

The tables are written by a script from the knowledge register of the Modding Manager, where each fact is kept once with its evidence and shared with the author's other mods. The ids of this page (L1, W14, X23 and so on) are kept as the records' aliases, so they still work in this mod's docs and tools, and `Find-HoiKnowledge.ps1 -Id W14` finds the record. The statuses and what each one means are in the register's `README.md`.

- **Add a fact** when a launch or an experiment teaches something: `Add-HoiKnowledge.ps1` in the Manager's `Scripts\Knowledge`, with `-Alias 1440p:<section letter><next number>` so that it appears in its table here, then `Export-HoiKnowledge.ps1 -Repo hoi4-1440p-ui-mod`.
- **Change a fact** in its record in the register, never between the markers on this page, and export again.
- What `tools/render_gui.py` draws of these facts is listed at the end of the page, under "What the renderer draws"; the harness notes of the H rows are in the facts themselves.
